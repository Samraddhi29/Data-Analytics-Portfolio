The project analyzes retail transaction data to understand customer behaviour and sales trends.
The goal was to identify top performing categories, key customer segments and monthly revenue patterns. 

## Dataset

The dataset was taken from [Kaggle-Retail Sales Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohammadtalib786/retail-sales-dataset).

## Tools used:
- Excel: Initial data exploration 
- SQL(MySQL): For analysis. 

## Key insights:
- Electronics and Clothing are the top revenue generating categories. 
- 55.8% of customers are aged 40 and above, making them the highest spending group. 
- Customers aged 20-29 spend mostly on Clothing and Beauty. 
- Sharp revenue drop between February and March, followed by steady growth from March to May possibly due to new product launches. 
- Revenue growth is seen the highest(97%) between October and November, indicating festive season
